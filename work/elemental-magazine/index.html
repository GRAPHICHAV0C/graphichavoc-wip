GRAPHIC HAVOC

Brand Identity + Art Direction + Editorial Design + Graphic Design + Typography by GRAPHIC HAVOC for:

ELEMENTAL MAGAZINE

Founded in Atlanta in 1998 by brothers Adam and Matt Wright, ELEMENTAL grew from a free local magazine covering underground hip-hop into a nationally distributed monthly publication, eventually moving its publishing operation to Brooklyn. By 2005, its circulation had reached 112,000.

GRAPHIC HAVOC began working with ELEMENTAL on Issue 38 in April 2002, developing a new identity and redesigning and art directing the magazine during its transition from a saddle-stitched to a perfect-bound format. Earlier issues had used the same graffiti-lettered masthead from cover to cover. The redesign introduced a flexible system that invited artists to reinterpret the ELEMENTAL name for individual issues, alongside the custom Hellemental typeface.

A recurring pair of downward arrows connected two visual traditions: the expressive vocabulary of graffiti lettering, including the idea that every writer has their own arrow articulated in the 1983 documentary Style Wars, and the utilitarian graphics of shipping labels and package handling. With subscriber copies mailed in clear poly bags, the cover identity also related to the experience of receiving the magazine as a delivered object. Across more than 13 issues, the arrows and recurring identity elements provided continuity while leaving room for distinct artistic interpretations.
