GRAPHIC HAVOC

Brand Identity Design + Creative Direction + Art Direction + Graphic Design + Motion Design + Web Design + Editorial Design by GRAPHIC HAVOC for:

CORE LIBRARY

In 2022, GRAPHIC HAVOC collaborated with award-winning filmmaker Deeyah Khan and Fuuse to create the visual identity system for CORE LIBRARY, a resource dedicated to understanding polarization, radicalization, and extremism.

Developed with support from the Norwegian government, CORE LIBRARY makes research and perspectives gathered through Fuuse's documentary work accessible for learning and study. Its focus is on the circumstances, processes, and human needs behind radicalization, rather than the outward image of extremist movements.

GRAPHIC HAVOC developed an adaptable identity spanning logo design, typography, color, photography guidelines, website design, motion design, and the CORE REPORT editorial publications. A circular CORE LIBRARY mark anchors the system; clear typography and a restrained palette support consistent communication across research, film, and print.
