GRAPHIC HAVOC

Brand Identity Design + Logo Design + Custom Typography + Graphic Design + Retail Branding by GRAPHIC HAVOC for:

BUDIES

BUDIES was conceived as an adult-use cannabis dispensary and gathering place in Williamsburg, Brooklyn, with a welcoming, human-centered atmosphere intended to feel like home.

GRAPHIC HAVOC developed a scalable brand identity centered on a custom hand-drawn logo and BUDIES OG, a bespoke typeface created for print, motion, and digital applications. The typeface incorporates brand-specific symbols, allowing the identity to extend consistently across formats and scales.

Rounded letterforms reference casual hand-painted signage and New York graffiti handstyles. A distinctive crossed mark, the phrase “marks the spot,” and graffiti-inspired cloud illustrations support a restrained green, white, and near-black palette. The system was developed for storefront signage, apparel, posters, packaging, and retail communications.
