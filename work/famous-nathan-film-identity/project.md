"Famous Nathan" is a documentary directed by Lloyd Handwerker, grandson of Nathan’s Famous founder Nathan Handwerker. Through family recollections, home movies, and archival material, the film traces the story of the Coney Island institution alongside a family’s immigrant experience and pursuit of the American dream.

GRAPHIC HAVOC developed the film’s identity system using the historic visual language of Nathan’s Famous as a point of departure. Storefront signage, menus, commercial lettering, and the restaurant’s early promotional graphics informed a typographic approach that connects the documentary to its subject without simply reproducing period artifacts.

The system encompasses a primary logotype, secondary typographic assemblages, additional logo and copy lockups, color, photographic treatments, compositing, and motion design. Distressed lettering, halftone imagery, paper textures, and red overprinting establish a consistent printed character across film graphics and marketing communications. Scale-specific assets and a detailed style guide support consistent application across print, digital, and motion.
